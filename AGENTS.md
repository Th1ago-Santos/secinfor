# Architecture
- Preventive maintenance uses shared domain helpers for effective status, filters and summaries in both the page and PDF, preventing divergent counts and overdue rules.
- PDF export consumes the already-authorized filtered page records and logs the export through the existing central audit RPC; database policies remain authoritative.
- Maintenance recurrence is generated by transactional database triggers and completed through a locking RPC; a unique origin index and durable generation marker prevent duplicate children even after deletion, while lineage UUIDs deliberately have no cascading foreign keys.
- Maintenance alerts, sidebar counts and section attention summaries share authorized query caches and domain derivation; dismissal hides active alerts while reading only reduces unread counts, avoiding divergent views.
